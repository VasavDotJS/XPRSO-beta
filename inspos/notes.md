# Inspiration notes

What I like about each image. Take the visual language, not the content: no logos, brand names, icons or text from these images.

- `01-glass-folder.jpg`: frosted, semi-transparent dark glass over light content, with soft depth. Use it for overlays such as the order bottom sheet and dialogs.
- `02-upload-card.jpg`: a big, very rounded white card on light grey with soft inner sections, full-pill buttons, and a single vivid blue used sparingly for the main action and progress. The bottom nav has a raised round centre "+" button.
- `03-pill-nav.jpg`: a pill-shaped segmented nav where the active item is a solid near-black pill with white text and the rest is dark text on white. Small pill chips show compact info (time, item, qty). Bold, legible labels with icons.
- `04-mono-controls.jpg`: a monochrome control set where selected states are glossy near-black rounded squares with a soft drop shadow and unselected states are flat light grey. Generous radius, Inter-style type. Use it for category chips, pagination, checkboxes and view toggles.
- `05-neumorphic-widget.jpg`: soft neumorphic grey panels with inset (pressed-in) wells and raised round buttons, plus a wide, extended display font for big numbers. Use the inset style for inputs and the PIN pad, and the display font for the grand total.

## Overall direction
- Calm, monochrome, soft-grey base. Near-black for selected and active states, white cards, and one accent colour (the blue from 02) used only for the primary action (e.g. Settle / Pay) and progress.
- Very rounded everything: pills for buttons and chips, 24–32 px radius for cards. Shadows are soft and diffuse, never harsh.
- Neumorphism and glass are accents, not the whole UI. Text, prices and totals must stay high-contrast (WCAG AA), even where the inspo is low-contrast grey on grey.
- Dark mode: derive it from 01's dark glass and the near-black states.
